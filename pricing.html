SHARED PLAN PRICING

Included people and additional places stay transparent.

Family and Business plans include a fixed number of people. If the owner needs more places, the additional-person price is charged for every extra person, every month.

For annual billing, the additional monthly price is multiplied by 12. For example, an additional place priced at $5 per month adds $60 to the annual total.

PRICING QUESTIONS

Before you choose.

Can a Free user join a paid family?

Yes. Their personal workspace can remain Free while the Family workspace gives them shared access.

When does a paid plan become active?

After the workspace owner submits payment details and any proof, Mushavo Budget finance staff review the request. Access begins when it is approved.

Will a price change alter an old invoice?

No. Every submitted subscription invoice stores the plan price and member calculation that applied at the time.
